# Privacy Policy — Select to Action

Last updated: 2026-08-24

Select to Action is a browser extension that lets you run quick actions
(Search, Copy, Translate, Dictionary, Currency conversion, and your own custom
actions) on text you select on a web page. This policy explains what the
extension does and does not do with your data.

## What data is handled

**Selected text.** When you choose an action, the text you selected is sent to
the third-party service that performs that action. This happens only when you
trigger an action, and only the selected text is sent.

- Translation: `translate.google.com` / `translate.googleapis.com`
- Currency conversion: `api.frankfurter.dev`, `open.er-api.com`
- Dictionary: `freedictionaryapi.com`

For Search and custom URL actions, the selected text is placed into the search
or URL you configured and opened in a new tab.

**Your settings.** Your preferences (default action, translation language,
currency pair, custom search engines, and custom actions) are stored using the
browser's `chrome.storage.sync` API. This keeps them on your own browser
profile and, if you are signed in to the browser, syncs them across your
devices through your browser account. These settings are not sent to us.

## What we do not do

- We do not run any server and do not receive, store, or log your data.
- We do not collect personally identifiable information, health, financial,
  authentication, or location data.
- We do not track your browsing, clicks, keystrokes, or mouse activity.
- We do not sell or transfer your data to third parties, and we do not use it
  for advertising, creditworthiness, or lending.

## Third-party services

Selected text sent to the translation, currency, and dictionary services above
is handled under each provider's own privacy policy at the time of the request.
The extension only contacts these services to perform the action you asked for.

## Custom actions

If you create custom JavaScript actions, they run code you wrote. You are
responsible for what your own custom actions do with the selected text or page.
